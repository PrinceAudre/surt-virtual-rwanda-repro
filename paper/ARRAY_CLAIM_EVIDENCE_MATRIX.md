## Pre-submission adversarial rules

1. Every manuscript number must be checked against the tracked evidence, not copied from memory.
2. `validation` must be qualified as software verification, computational cross-validation, product/source validation, or external/observational validation.
3. `portable`, `reproducible`, and `generalizable` must specify the tested dimension.
4. Any statement containing `first`, `unique`, `novel algorithm`, `superior`, `best`, `low-resource`, `lightweight`, `validated hazard`, or `validated exposure` is presumptively blocked unless new direct evidence is added.
5. A comparator's missing feature may not be asserted from absence in a quick search. Prefer scope descriptions supported by primary documentation.
6. The manuscript must not describe v1.4.0 as released until DOI, tag, archive, checksum manifest and exact commit are synchronized.
7. The current University of Rwanda affiliation identifies the author, not institutional sponsorship or endorsement.
8. The absence of an exact integrated match in the documented prior-art search is not evidence of a world-first claim.
9. African/LMIC relevance is a deployment rationale only unless comparative operational evidence is added; CDT and DHIS2 already demonstrate much stronger African institutional deployment.
10. `contract-first` is descriptive framing, not a priority claim; AutoGIS and broader data-contract work already use explicit contracts in geospatial systems.
11. A benchmark demonstrates only the declared workload, machine and metric; Climate-CAFE and the Climate Econometrics Toolkit already provide performance precedent in adjacent climate-processing software.
12. Robust wrapper behavior is not novelty by itself; spatcovar, GDAL and other mature tools already package CRS, geometry, missing-data and coverage-aware zonal operations.
